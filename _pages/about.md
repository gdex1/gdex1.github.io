I specialize in large-scale optimization and algorithmic decision-making, developing algorithms that integrate predictive models with optimization to guide decisions and resource allocation under complex constraints. My work spans mathematical optimization, causal policy evaluation, and the engineering of production systems that translate these methods into economically consequential decisions.

I am currently a Staff Machine Learning Engineer at Nubank, where I serve as Tech Lead for Algorithmic Decision Systems in AI Core. I drive a portfolio of research and engineering projects developing shared optimization and evaluation capabilities across financial products, including lending, credit, and deposit pricing.

Previously, I was a Senior Applied Research Scientist at LinkedIn, where I worked on large-scale mathematical optimization, algorithmic advertising, and LLM inference efficiency.

I completed my PhD at Purdue University under the supervision of [Professor Petros Drineas](https://www.cs.purdue.edu/homes/pdrineas/) and [Professor Rajiv Khanna](https://rjvak7.github.io/). My doctoral research centered on theoretical topics in optimization and randomized matrix algorithms with applications to machine learning.
